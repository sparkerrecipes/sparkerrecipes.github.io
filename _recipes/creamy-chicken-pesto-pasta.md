Notes: This was good but just a little on the meager side. I've scaled up the amount of chicken, penne, and pesto. It may also need more sun dried tomatoes, but it probably doesn't need more heavy cream

### Source
[Mediterranean Dish](https://www.themediterraneandish.com/creamy-chicken-pesto-pasta/)

### Ingredients
* 1.75 boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1-inch pieces
* 2 tspns Italian seasoning
* Kosher salt
* Black pepper
* 10 ounces penne pasta
* 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
* 1/2 cup heavy cream
* 1/4 cup parmesan cheese, plus more for serving if you like
* 1/4 cup julienned sun dried tomatoes, drained if packed in oil
* 3/4 cup basil pesto
* 1/2 to 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes


### Directions
1. Add the chicken to a bowl and season with the Italian seasoning and a big pinch each of kosher salt and black pepper. Toss to coat.
1. Bring a medium pot of water to a boil and salt it well with about 1 tablespoon of salt. Drop the pasta in the boiling water and cook until al dente according to package instructions (about 8 to 9 minutes).
1. While the pasta is cooking, add the olive oil to a large non-stick skillet and heat over medium-high. Add the chicken and cook, tossing occasionally, until golden brown on the outside and cooked through, about 7 to 8 minutes.
1. When the pasta is ready, reserve about 1 cup of the cooking water and drain.
1. To the pan with the chicken, lower the heat and stir in the cooked pasta, heavy cream, parmesan, and sundried tomatoes. Stir until everything is well combined. If it looks a bit dry, add a splash of the pasta cooking water (a couple tablespoons at a time) until the pasta is glossy and coated in the creamy sauce.
1. Turn off the heat and take the pan off the burner. Stir in the basil pesto and finish with a sprinkle of grated parmesan, red pepper flakes, and basil leaves. Serve.
