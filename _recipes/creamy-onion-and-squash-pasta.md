### Source
[First Mess](https://thefirstmess.com/2026/08/05/caramelized-onion-yellow-squash-pasta/#recipe)

### Ingredients
* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* 1 medium Vidalia onion, sliced
* 3 medium yellow squash (465 grams), sliced
* 1 clove garlic, sliced
* salt and pepper, to taste
* ⅓ cup raw cashews, soaked for at least 2 hours and drained
* 1 cup water
* 1 tablespoon white miso
* 1 tablespoon lemon juice
* ½ cup fresh basil leaves, torn or chopped
* 454 grams (1 lb) dried pasta

### Directions
* Place a large skillet over medium heat. Once hot, pour in the olive oil. Add the sliced onions and stir. Lower the heat to medium-low and keep cooking until the onions have shrunk down considerably and are starting to take on a light golden hue, about 20 minutes. Stir often.
* Then, add the yellow squash and sliced garlic, and stir. Bring the heat up to just under medium. Season liberally with salt and pepper. Continue cooking the onions and yellow squash, stirring often, until vegetables are jammy, light golden brown, and nicely caramelized, about 30 minutes. If the pan is drying up and vegetables are browning too fast, add splashes of water.
* Add the caramelized onions, yellow squash and garlic mixture to the blender along with the drained cashews, water, miso, and lemon juice. Blend this mixture on high until completely smooth. Check the sauce base for seasoning and add more salt, pepper, or lemon if you like.
* Cook the pasta according to package directions in salted boiling water. Before draining, reserve 1 cup of the pasta cooking water.
* Return the large skillet to the stove over medium heat. Pour in the blended caramelized onion and yellow squash sauce and add the cooked pasta. Stir to combine, adding more pasta water if necessary to loosen the mixture up.
* Stir in the basil leaves and serve immediately! I like to finish this with lots of black pepper.
