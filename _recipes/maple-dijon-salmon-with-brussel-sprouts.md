A sheet-pan dinner: the sprouts get a head start, then the glazed salmon joins them for the last 10 minutes.

### Source
Claude

### Ingredients
• 2 pounds Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
• 3 tablespoons olive oil
• 1.5 teaspoons kosher salt, divided
• 0.5 teaspoons black pepper
• 4 salmon fillets (about 6 oz each)
• 4 tablespoons maple syrup
• 3 tablespoons Dijon mustard
• 2 teaspoons whole-grain mustard (optional)
• 2 garlic clove, grated
• 2 teaspoons lemon juice

### Instructions
1. Preheat: Heat the oven to 425°F and line a rimmed sheet pan with parchment.
2. Roast the sprouts: Toss 2 pounds Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved with 3 tablespoons olive oil, half of the 1.5 teaspoons kosher salt, divided, and 0.5 teaspoons black pepper. Spread them cut-side down on the pan and roast until the undersides are browning.
3. Make the glaze: While the sprouts roast, whisk together 4 tablespoons maple syrup, 3 tablespoons Dijon mustard, 2 teaspoons whole-grain mustard (optional), 2 garlic clove, grated, and 2 teaspoons lemon juice.
4. Add the salmon: Pat 4 salmon fillets (about 6 oz each) dry and season with the rest of the salt. Push the sprouts to the edges of the pan, lay the salmon in the middle skin-side down, and brush most of the glaze over the top.
5. Finish roasting: Roast until the salmon flakes easily and reads 125–130°F in the thickest part.
6. Serve: Brush the remaining glaze over the salmon and toss the sprouts in any glaze that dripped onto the pan.
