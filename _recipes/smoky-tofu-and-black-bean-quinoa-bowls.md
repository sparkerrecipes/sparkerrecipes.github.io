Five make-ahead lunches: smoky baked tofu, roasted sweet potato, black beans, and corn over quinoa, with a quick blender salsa.

### Ingredients
* 14 ounces extra-firm tofu, pressed and cut into 3/4-inch cubes
* 1 tablespoons cornstarch
* 1 tablespoons tamari (gluten-free soy sauce)
* 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
* 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
* 1.5 teaspoons ground cumin
* 1 teaspoons chili powder
* 0.5 teaspoons garlic powder
* 1 teaspoons kosher salt, divided
* 2 medium sweet potatoes, cut into 3/4-inch cubes
* 1 cups dry quinoa, rinsed
* 2 cups water
* 1 can black beans (15 oz), drained and rinsed
* 1 cups frozen corn, thawed

### Ingredients - Dressing
* ¾ cup plain Greek yogurt (or unsweetened dairy-free yogurt)
* 2 tbsp mayo
* 1–2 chipotles in adobo, minced, plus 1 tsp of the adobo sauce
* Juice of 1 lime
* 1 small garlic clove, grated
* 1 tsp dried dill
* 1 tsp dried chives or 1 tbsp fresh
* ½ tsp onion powder
* Salt to taste
* Water to thin, 1 tbsp at a time
* Small handful of cilantro

### Instructions
1. Preheat: Heat the oven to 425°F and line a large sheet pan with parchment.
2. Season the tofu: In a bowl, toss 14 ounces extra-firm tofu, pressed and cut into 3/4-inch cubes with 1 tablespoons tamari (gluten-free soy sauce) and 1 tablespoon of the 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided. Then add 1 tablespoons cornstarch, 2 teaspoons smoked paprika, 1.5 teaspoons ground cumin, 1 teaspoons chili powder, 0.5 teaspoons garlic powder, and half of the 1 teaspoons kosher salt, divided, tossing gently until everything is coated.
3. Prep the sweet potatoes: Toss 2 medium sweet potatoes, cut into 3/4-inch cubes with the remaining olive oil and a pinch of the remaining salt. Spread the sweet potatoes on one half of the pan and the tofu on the other, without crowding.
4. Roast: Roast, flipping everything halfway through, until the tofu is firm and golden and the sweet potatoes are tender and browned at the edges.
5. Cook the quinoa: Meanwhile, bring 1 cups dry quinoa, rinsed, 2 cups water, and a pinch of salt to a boil. Cover and simmer on low until the water is absorbed.
6. Rest the quinoa: Take the quinoa off the heat, keep it covered, and let it steam. Then fluff with a fork.
7. Make the dressing: Blend all ingredients together.
8. Assemble: Let everything cool. Divide the quinoa among 5 containers and top with the tofu, sweet potatoes, 1 can black beans (15 oz), drained and rinsed, and 1 cups frozen corn, thawed. Pack the salsa in small separate containers.

### Notes
The bowls keep 4–5 days in the fridge. Reheat the bowl, then add the salsa cold. If you want something fresh on top, add half an avocado or a handful of greens on the day you eat it rather than at prep time. The tofu is crispiest when you reheat it in a toaster oven or air fryer, but the microwave works fine.
