### Ingredients
* 2 pounds small yellow potatoes
* 2 teaspoons sea salt, divided
* 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for the pans
* 1½ teaspoons garlic powder
* ¾ teaspoon onion powder
* Freshly ground black pepper
* 1 tablespoon finely chopped fresh herbs, parsley, chives, and/or dill

### Instructions
0. Preheat the oven to 425°F and liberally spray 2 baking sheets with olive oil.
0. Place the potatoes and 1 teaspoon of the salt in a large pot and fill it with enough water to cover the potatoes by 1 inch. Bring to a boil and cook until the potatoes are soft and fork-tender, 15 to 20 minutes.
0. Drain potatoes then return to pot. Add olive oil and spices and roughly mix, aiming to build up a little potato paste on the pieces.
0. Arrange on the baking sheets, gently smash with a masher, and spray with oil one more time.
0. Roast 30-40 minutes, rotating halfway.
