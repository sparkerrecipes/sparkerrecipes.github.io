### Ingredients
* 4-5 lbs skin-on chicken thighs / chicken parts

### Ingredients - Lemongrass Marinade
* 2 tablespoons roughly chopped, peeled fresh ginger
* 6 garlic cloves
* 1 (5-inch) piece lemongrass, tough outer layers removed, roughly chopped
* 2 tablespoons honey
* ¼ cup neutral oil, such as vegetable or avocado oil, plus more for brushing
* 1 tablespoon kosher salt

### Ingredients - Sriracha-Brown Sugar Sauce
* ¼ cup packed brown sugar
* ¼ cup soy sauce or tamari
* 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
* 1 teaspoon Sriracha
* ½ teaspoon sesame oil
* 1 teaspoon minced peeled fresh ginger
* 1 tablespoon minced garlic

### Ingredients - Scallion Oil
* 1 bunch scallions, thinly sliced
* 2 teaspoons minced peeled fresh ginger
* 2 teaspoons minced garlic
* 1 tablespoon soy sauce or tamari
* 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
* 1 tablespoon fish sauce
* 2 tablespoons neutral oil, like vegetable or avocado oil

### Instructions
0. Heat the grill to medium-high, or heat oven to 425.
0. Make the lemongrass paste. In a food processor, combine the ginger, garlic, lemongrass, honey, oil, and salt. Blend until a paste forms.
0. Pat the chicken dry and rub it all over with the lemongrass paste until evenly coated. 
0. (GRILL) Reduce the heat to medium and brush the grates with oil. Add the chicken directly to the grill, breast side up, over direct heat and cook until lightly charred, about 5 minutes. Reduce the heat to low, flip the chicken, cover the grill and cook until the juices run clear and the internal temperature of the thigh reads 165°F on an instant-read thermometer, about 45 minutes. Let the chicken rest for 10 minutes before carving.
0. (OVEN) Place chicken pieces on parchment paper. Roast for 15 minutes at 425, then reduce heat to 350 and roast another 20-30 minutes
0. Meanwhile, make the sriracha-brown sugar sauce. In a small saucepan, combine the brown sugar, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sriracha, sesame oil, ginger, and garlic. Bring to a simmer over medium heat. Cook until the sauce thickens slightly, about 10 minutes. Remove from heat. The sauce will continue to thicken as it cools.
0. Make the scallion oil. In a small bowl, whisk together scallions, ginger, garlic, soy sauce, rice vinegar, fish sauce, and oil until combined.
0. Serve the chicken drizzled with sriracha-brown sugar sauce and scallion oil, or serve them on the side for dipping.
