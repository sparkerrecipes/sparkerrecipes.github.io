### Source
[Mediterranean Dish](https://www.themediterraneandish.com/lettuce-salad-recipe/)

### Ingredients
* Enough romaine for four servings (at jewel, this is two of the organic packs)
* 2 shallots, thinly sliced
* 2 lemons, zested and juiced
* 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
* Kosher salt and black pepper
* Crushed red pepper flakes, or Aleppo pepper for a milder taste
* 1 cup finely grated parmesan cheese

### Directions
0. Add the lettuce, shallots, and lemon zest to a bowl.
0. Season with kosher salt, black pepper, lemon juice, and olive oil. Toss to combine.
0. Add the Parmesan cheese and red pepper flakes (optional) and toss again. Enjoy.
