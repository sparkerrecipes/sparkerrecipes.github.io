Serve over polenta.

### Source
[Source](https://www.simplyrecipes.com/pan-roasted-shrimp-and-okra-recipe-8678286)

### Ingredients
* 1 1/4 pounds peeled and deveined large raw shrimp
* 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
* 2 1/2 teaspoons Cajun seasoning, divided
* 3 tablespoons canola oil, divided
* 8 ounces fresh okra, halved lengthwise
* 1 pint cherry tomatoes (about 10 ounces or 2 cups) 
* 1 1/2 teaspoons fresh lemon juice (from 1 lemon)
* Chopped fresh parsley (optional)

### Instructions
0. Heat a large cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat for 2 minutes. Meanwhile, pat the shrimp dry with paper towels and toss with salt and 1 1/2 teaspoons of the Cajun seasoning; set aside.
0. Add 1 tablespoon of the oil, okra, and 1/2 teaspoon of the Cajun seasoning to the hot skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the okra is charred in spots and tender, 4 to 5 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
0. Add 1 tablespoon of the oil, cherry tomatoes, and 1/2 teaspoon of the Cajun seasoning to the hot skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes begin to burst and blister, about 3 minutes. Transfer to a separate plate.
0. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon of oil and the shrimp to the hot skillet. Cook, stirring often, until opaque and pink, 3 to 4 minutes.
0. Return the tomatoes to the skillet with the lemon juice, stir to combine, then stir in the okra. Garnish with parsley, if using.
