### Ingredients
* 1.5 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs (or 4 bone-in thighs)
* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* 3 garlic cloves, grated
* 1 lemon, zested (save the juice for serving)
* 2 teaspoons dried oregano
* 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
* 0.5 teaspoons ground coriander
* 1.3 teaspoons kosher salt
* 0.5 teaspoons black pepper
* 1 teaspoon honey

### Directions - Boneless
0. Preheat with the pan in: Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C) with a sheet pan inside. Pat 1.5 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs dry with paper towels and unfold them so they lie flat.
0. Make the paste: Stir together 2 tablespoons olive oil, 3 garlic cloves, grated, 1 lemon, zested (save the juice for serving), 2 teaspoons dried oregano, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, 0.5 teaspoons ground coriander, 1.3 teaspoons kosher salt, 0.5 teaspoons black pepper, and 1 teaspoon honey into a loose paste.
0. Coat and lay out: Toss the thighs in the paste until fully coated. Pull the hot pan from the oven, lay the thighs out with space between them, smooth side up.
0. Roast: Roast until the edges are browned and the thickest part reads 170–175°F. Boneless thighs are forgiving, so err on the higher side.
0. Broil to char: Switch to broil and cook until spots of char appear on top. Watch it closely — the honey goes from brown to burnt fast.
0. Rest and finish: Rest 5 minutes, squeeze the reserved lemon juice over the top, and spoon the pan juices over the rice.

### Directions - Bone-in
0. Preheat and dry the chicken: Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Pat 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs very dry with paper towels — dry skin is what gets crisp. If you have time, salt them and leave uncovered in the fridge for 30 minutes to an hour.
0. Make the paste: Stir together 2 tablespoons olive oil, 3 garlic cloves, grated, 1 lemon, zested (save the juice for serving), 2 teaspoons dried oregano, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, 0.5 teaspoons ground coriander, 1.5 teaspoons kosher salt, 0.5 teaspoons black pepper, and 1 teaspoon honey into a loose paste.
0. Coat: Rub the paste all over the thighs, working some under the skin where you can. Arrange skin-side up on a foil-lined sheet pan or in a cast-iron skillet, leaving space between them.
0. Roast: Roast until the skin is deep golden and crisp and the thickest part reads 175°F (they're better a bit past 165°F), about 40 minutes (check at 35). If the skin isn't as brown as you'd like, broil for the last few minutes
0. Rest and finish: Rest 5 minutes, then squeeze the reserved lemon juice over the top and spoon any pan juices over the rice.
