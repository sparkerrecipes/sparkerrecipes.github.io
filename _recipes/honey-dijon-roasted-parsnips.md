### Source
[The Mediterranean Dish](https://www.themediterraneandish.com/honey-dijon-roasted-parsnips-with-hazelnuts/)

### Ingredients
* 2 pounds parsnips, peeled and trimmed
* 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
* 1 teaspoon salt
* 1 tablespoon honey
* 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
* 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar

### Directions
1. Preheat the oven to 400ºF. Divide the thick top of the parsnips from the slender ends. Cut the thickest part lengthwise into halves or quarters (they should be about 1 inch wide at the thickest part). All the pieces should closely match in size.
1. (Optional) Parboil the parsnips for 6 minutes
1. Line a baking pan with parchment paper. Add parsnips, drizzle with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Toss to coat.
1. Roast for 25 minutes. Make the glaze by mixing the honey, mustard, and vinegar.
1. Remove the parsnips, toss with the glaze, and roast for another 10-15 minutes until brown and glazed.
